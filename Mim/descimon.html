Professor Henri Descimon

Henri Descimon is one of the world's experts on European butterflies. In recent years he has increasingly concentrated on Asian montane butterflies.

Here he meets with Michael C. Singer in an unidentified coniferous woodland in the South of France. (Photo courtesy of Camille Parmesan and Mike Singer)

RIP: My good friend Henri died in Marseille on 1 January 2025. I will miss his enthusiasm for butterflies, species, and evolutionary genetics. And his sense of humour. He used wonderful words, like "coabitation", meaning sympatry, but also echoing the newspaper term used for President Mitterrand's living in with his mistress.

Henri died at 90, which is also the same age that Alfred Russel Wallace died. I hope to live that long too!
